# NairaLeap Insurance Portal — Product and Regulatory Source Notes

**Checked:** 12 October 2026

**Purpose:** Evidence for discovery-stage product preferences and a non-binding quote-request workflow. These notes are not financial, legal or insurance advice, and do not establish a provider relationship.

## Implementation boundary

The portal records an applicant's contact details, individual/organization choice, cover category, location and relevant product preferences. It does **not** collect identity documents, BVN, bank or pension account data, beneficiary identity documents, detailed medical history, or document uploads during initial discovery. It does not compute rates, estimate annuity payouts, recommend a policy, bind cover, accept premiums or submit an insurer application.

Provider preferences are not referrals or partnership claims. NairaLeap has no confirmed insurer quote API or appointed-provider agreement in this repository. Any formal application, eligibility decision, price, illustration, product availability, licensing status, underwriting or terms must be confirmed directly with the insurer through its authorized process.

## Regulatory and provider-verification sources

- NAICOM's [minimum-capital compliance list](https://naicom.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/List-of-Insurance-Companies-that-Complied-with-the-MCR-Prescribed-by-NIIRA-2025.pdf) is dated **31 July 2026**. Its rows identify Leadway Assurance Company Limited (LIC 002, Composite), AIICO Insurance Plc (LIC 006, Composite), Industrial and General Insurance Plc (LIC 038, Composite), and Stanbic IBTC Insurance Limited (LIC 018, Life).
- That document concerns minimum-capital compliance under NIIRA 2025. It is **not, by itself, proof of a current operating licence, product approval, current availability, or NairaLeap appointment/partnership**.
- NAICOM's [life-insurer directory](https://naicom.gov.ng/life/) and [regulatory instruments](https://naicom.gov.ng/regulatory-instruments/) pages returned only a site shell in the rendered and extracted views; the life-company directory table was not available for verification in this review. NAICOM's [public insurers portal page](https://portal.naicom.gov.ng/Public/Public_Insurers.aspx) describes product registration, direct or authorized-broker sales, insurer policy-data recording and agent/broker authorization, but its public content did not expose named insurer entries. No active-licence conclusion is encoded in NairaLeap.
- NAICOM's official search-index listing identifies **“Regulatory Requirements for Underwriting of Annuity Business in Nigeria – 1st February 2025”** on its [circular index](https://naicom.gov.ng/naicom-circulars/). The linked PDF supplied in the project brief could not be retrieved because the storage host returned a TLS certificate-name mismatch; the index itself exposed only the site shell. Accordingly, the circular has **not** been used to hardcode legal or underwriting requirements. Obtain and review a current official copy before enforcing provider onboarding controls.
- AIICO's 2019 application form includes identity, BVN, PEP, source-of-funds and health declarations; these were treated as provider-specific, formal-stage data from a dated form—not current universal statutory requirements.
- The official [NIIRA 2025 PDF](https://naicom.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/NIIRA-2025.pdf) reviewed in this research is internally labelled a Bill and leaves commencement blank. It was not treated as sufficient proof of current operative law or used to hardcode retail KYC requirements.

## Portal controls and remaining dependencies

1. **No ratings or ranking:** no independently comparable, verified customer-rating dataset is connected, so providers are not ranked.
2. **No guaranteed return or rate claims:** the portal shows only carefully attributed facts from product pages and never computes an expected or guaranteed return, premium, annuity rate or maturity amount.
3. **No document intake at discovery:** provider-specific formal-application document lists remain source notes and do not become required fields in the initial enquiry.
4. **Catalogue coverage is partial:** motor, health, agricultural, group and commercial products remain supported as enquiry categories, but no product/provider snapshot is advertised until current official source evidence is checked.
5. **Manual provider verification remains necessary:** confirm active licence, product authorization, geographic/eligibility limits, product wording, live price/illustration, accepted documents, claims channel, payment channel and quote validity before a provider is selected or a formal application begins.
6. **No live provider integrations were found:** request submission persists to NairaLeap's existing authenticated request queue; staff/provider follow-up remains manual.
